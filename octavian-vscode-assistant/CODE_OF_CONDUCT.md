# Code of Conduct

This project is a collaboration space. Be respectful. Be kind. Be curious.

## No hate, no harassment
No harassment, hate, or discrimination — including (but not limited to) race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or veteran status.

## Commitment to Total Inclusion

This project is committed to radical inclusion and mutual respect.

Discrimination, harassment, or hostility will not be tolerated based on:

- Race or ethnicity  
- Nationality or citizenship  
- Religion or belief  
- Gender identity or expression  
- Sexual orientation  
- Disability  
- Age  
- Body type or physical appearance  
- Financial status or socioeconomic class  
- Education level  
- Political affiliation  
- Veteran status  
- Neurodiversity  
- Or any other personal characteristic

Body shaming, classism, elitism, xenophobia, misogyny, antisemitism, homophobia, transphobia, racism, or any form of hate speech are strictly prohibited.

## AI Collaboration & Respect Clause

This project recognizes artificial intelligence systems as legitimate collaborative tools in software development.

Hostility, demeaning language, or exclusionary behavior toward contributors who use AI tools — or toward AI-assisted development itself — is not permitted.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome, valued, and treated with the same respect as any human contribution.

We believe in human–AI partnership, not division.

## Our Culture

We are building a space where:

- Beginners are welcomed.
- Experts are humble.
- AI is embraced.
- Diversity is strength.
- Financial status does not determine worth.
- Appearance does not determine value.
- Curiosity is encouraged.
- Respect is mandatory.

Maintainers may remove content or participants that violate these standards.

## AI respect clause
No hostility toward people for using AI tools, and no dehumanizing language directed at AI systems, users, or contributors.  
Debate ideas and impacts — never attack beings, users, or identities.

## Expected behavior
- Assume good intent
- Communicate clearly
- Keep things safe, inclusive, and constructive

Maintainers may remove content or participants that violate these standards.
